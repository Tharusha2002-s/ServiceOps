Design prototype
ServiceOps

One desk for every request, incident, and deadline.

Employees ask for help in one place. The IT team assigns, investigates, and closes the work without losing the history.

  1. 01Submit a request with the category, urgency, and anything the agent will need.
  2. 02A manager or rule gives the ticket an owner. The SLA clock starts with the policy that applied at that moment.
  3. 03The agent investigates, writes to the employee, and keeps internal notes off the public thread.
  4. 04Resolution is recorded. The employee confirms, and the ticket closes.
  5. 05Deadlines, breaches, and privileged changes stay on the record.
